About this dashboard

This dashboard tracks the Lithuanian economy through the indicators that economists, analysts and journalists follow most closely: real GDP and the expenditure components behind its growth, industrial production, retail trade and sentiment, unemployment, employment, job vacancies and wages, and consumer price inflation (HICP), including core inflation and its main components. Where the data allow it, Lithuania is shown next to Latvia, Estonia and the euro area, so that each reading can be judged against its neighbours rather than in isolation.

The data come from Eurostat and Statistics Lithuania and are downloaded again every Monday; growth rates are computed from the published levels and checked against Eurostat's own. Every chart can be switched to a table or downloaded as CSV, and the release calendar lists the next scheduled publications. The dashboard contains no forecasts.

This is a personal project by Stefan Girstmair. It is not an official publication of Lietuvos bankas or any other institution, and it is not endorsed by them. For official statistics, see Statistics Lithuania and Eurostat; for the forecasts and analysis of Lietuvos bankas, see its website.

Sources: Eurostat and Statistics Lithuania (Oficialiosios statistikos portalas). Growth rates are computed from the published levels and checked against Eurostat's own rates.
